## A. Grammar (Q1-6)

1. Neither of the options ___ correct. (is/are)
2. He is senior ___ me. (than/to)
3. She congratulated me ___ my success. (for/on)
4. I look forward ___ you. (to meet / to meeting)
5. The news ___ shocking. (is/are)
6. One of my friends ___ a bike. (has/have)

## B. Phrase / Vocab (Q7-12)

7. **discuss about** → a) discuss b) discuss on c) no change
8. **more smarter** → a) smarter b) most smart c) no change
9. **Reluctant** = a) unwilling b) eager c) loud
10. **Meticulous** = a) careful b) careless c) angry
11. **Integrity** = a) correctness/honesty b) speed c) size
12. **Obsolete** = a) outdated b) new c) bright

---

## ✅ Answers

1. **is**  2. **to**  3. **on**  4. **to meeting**  5. **is**  6. **has**
7. **a**  8. **a**  9. **a**  10. **a**  11. **a**  12. **a**
