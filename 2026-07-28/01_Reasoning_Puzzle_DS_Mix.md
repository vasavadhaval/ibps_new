## 1. ✍️ Floor Puzzle (8 min)

**A, B, C, D, E** — 5 floors (1=ground, 5=top).

1. C lives on floor 2.
2. B lives immediately above C.
3. E lives on the top floor.
4. A does not live on floor 1.
5. D lives below A.

**Questions:**
1. Who on floor 1?
2. Floor of A?
3. Who immediately below E?

---

## 2. ✍️ Linear Puzzle (7 min)

**P, Q, R, S, T** — left → right, facing North.

1. P sits at left end.
2. T sits at right end.
3. Q sits immediate right of P.
4. S sits between Q and R.

**Questions:**
4. Position 3?
5. Who between S and T?
6. Who sits 2nd from right?

## 3. ✍️ Data Sufficiency (Q7-10)

7. What is age of A?  
   I: A is 5 years older than B.  
   II: B is 20 years old.  
   Sufficient?

8. Who is tallest among P, Q, R?  
   I: P > Q  
   II: Q > R  
   Sufficient?

9. What is the number?  
   I: It is even.  
   II: It lies between 10 and 14.  
   Sufficient?

10. What day is today?  
    I: Yesterday was Monday.  
    II: Tomorrow is Wednesday.  
    Sufficient?

## 4. Quick (Q11-12)

12. Rank 13th top, 20th bottom. Total?
