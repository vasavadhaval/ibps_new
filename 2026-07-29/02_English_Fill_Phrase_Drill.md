## A. Fill in the Blanks (Q1-6)

1. He is junior ___ me. (than / to)
2. Success depends ___ hard work. (on / at)
3. She has been working ___ Monday. (since / for)
4. I prefer coffee ___ tea. (than / to)
5. He was ___ tired that he slept early. (so / such)
6. ___ honest man is respected. (A / An)

## B. Phrase Replacement (Q7-12)

7. Please **discuss about** the plan.  
   a) discuss  b) discuss on  c) no change

8. She is **capable to** do this.  
   a) capable of doing  b) capable for  c) no change

9. I look forward **to meet** you.  
   a) to meeting  b) for meet  c) no change

10. He is **more taller** than Shyam.  
    a) taller  b) most taller  c) no change

11. **Return back** the form.  
    a) Return  b) Give back again  c) no change

12. The news **are** shocking.  
    a) is  b) were  c) no change

---

## ✅ Answers

1. **to**  2. **on**  3. **since**  4. **to**  5. **so**  6. **An**
7. **a**  8. **a**  9. **a**  10. **a**  11. **a**  12. **a**
