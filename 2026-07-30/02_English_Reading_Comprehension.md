> Week 7 RC revision. Banking + IT theme.

---

## Passage

Core banking solutions allow customers to access their accounts from any branch or digital channel. Transactions are updated in a central database in real time. This improves convenience and reduces paperwork. At the same time, banks must invest in cybersecurity because online fraud has increased. Multi-factor authentication and encryption help protect customer data. Regulators expect banks to maintain strong controls and report suspicious activity quickly. Technology creates efficiency, but trust depends on safe systems and informed customers.

---

## ✍️ Questions

1. Main idea?
   - a) Close all branches  b) Core banking helps access but needs strong security  c) Paperwork should increase  d) Regulators are useless

2. Transactions in core banking are updated:
   - a) Once a year  b) In real time (central DB)  c) Never  d) Only on paper

3. Why invest in cybersecurity?
   - a) Online fraud increased  b) To slow customers  c) To stop UPI forever  d) No reason

4. What helps protect data?
   - a) MFA and encryption  b) Sharing OTP  c) Weak passwords  d) Ignoring updates

5. Synonym of **suspicious** (here):
   - a) doubtful/shady  b) safe  c) happy  d) official

6. Inference: Trust needs:
   - a) Only marketing  b) Safe systems + informed customers  c) No technology  d) No regulators

---

## ✅ Answers

1. **b**  2. **b**  3. **a**  4. **a**  5. **a**  6. **b**
