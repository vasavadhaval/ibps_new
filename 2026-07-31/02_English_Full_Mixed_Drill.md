## Error (Q1-5)

1. (A) Neither of the boys / (B) are / (C) late. / (D) No error
2. (A) He is senior / (B) than / (C) me. / (D) No error
3. (A) She congratulated me / (B) for / (C) my win. / (D) No error
4. (A) The news / (B) are / (C) good. / (D) No error
5. (A) I look forward / (B) to meet / (C) you. / (D) No error

## Fill (Q6-10)

6. Success depends ___ hard work. (on/at)
7. She has been here ___ Monday. (since/for)
8. I prefer tea ___ coffee. (than/to)
9. He is good ___ English. (in/at)
10. ___ European country. (a/an)

## Phrase / Vocab (Q11-15)

11. **discuss about** → a) discuss b) no change
12. **more taller** → a) taller b) no change
13. **Phishing** = a) fraud to steal info b) sport
14. **Encrypt** = a) code/hide data b) delete
15. **Obsolete** = a) outdated b) new

## ✅ Answers

1. (B) are→**is**  2. (B) than→**to**  3. (B) for→**on**  4. (B) are→**is**  5. (B) to meet→**to meeting**
6. **on**  7. **since**  8. **to**  9. **at**  10. **a**
11. **a**  12. **a**  13. **a**  14. **a**  15. **a**
