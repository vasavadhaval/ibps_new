# 📖 English — Fix-list Drill | Day 45

> Kal ke English misses. Target: **12+ / 14**.

---

## A. Error / SVA (Q1-5)

1. One of my friends ___ here. (live / lives)
2. Neither of the boys ___ ready. (is / are)
3. The news ___ good. (is / are)
4. Each of the girls ___ a bag. (has / have)
5. The furniture ___ costly. (is / are)

## B. Prepositions (Q6-10)

6. He is senior ___ me. (than / to)
7. I prefer tea ___ coffee. (than / to)
8. She congratulated me ___ my success. (for / on)
9. She is good ___ English. (in / at)
10. Success depends ___ hard work. (on / at)

## C. Phrase (Q11-14)

11. **discuss about** → correct form?
12. **return back** → ?
13. **more taller** → ?
14. **capable to do** → ?

---

## ✅ Answers

1. **lives**  2. **is**  3. **is**  4. **has**  5. **is**
6. **to**  7. **to**  8. **on**  9. **at**  10. **on**
11. **discuss**  12. **return**  13. **taller**  14. **capable of doing**

---

## Wall list (2 min roz)
| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| one of … live | one of … **lives** |
| senior than | senior **to** |
| prefer than | prefer **to** |
| congratulate for | congratulate **on** |
| good in English | good **at** |
| discuss about | **discuss** |
| return back | **return** |
