# 🧠 Reasoning Weak — Batch A | 22 Aug

> Syllogism No+All · Direction · Blood · Calendar · Alphanumeric  
> Target: **10+ / 12**

---

## 1. Syllogism — No + All (rule)
- No A is B. All B are C.  
  → **Some C are not A** (usually follows)  
  → **No A is C** often **does NOT** follow  

---

## 2. Direction — Displacement
East then North then West (same East distance) → leftover = North distance.

---

## 3. Blood Relation — Pointers
“Only son of my mother’s father” = **maternal uncle**.

Family: A brother of B; B daughter of C; D father of A → C is **wife** of D (mother).

---

## 4. Calendar
Day before yesterday = Friday → Today = Sunday → Day after tomorrow = **Tuesday**.

---

## 5. Alphanumeric counting
“Preceded by X but not followed by Y” → check each match carefully; count only valid triples.

---

## ✍️ Practice

1. No pen is book. All books are papers. Conclusion: Some papers are not pens — Follows?
2. No pen is book. All books are papers. Conclusion: No pen is paper — Follows?
3. 8 km East, 6 km North, 8 km West. Distance from start?
4. “He is the only son of my mother’s father.” Relation to speaker (female)?
5. A brother of B. B daughter of C. D father of A. C related to D?
6. Day before yesterday Thursday. Day after tomorrow?
7. How many 4’s preceded by 2 but not followed by 5? Series: 2 4 5 2 4 7 2 4 5 2 4 8
8. Rank 9th left, 17th right. Total?
9. Codes: All dogs cats. Some cats animals. Some dogs animals — Follows?
10. 5 km South, right, 12 km. Distance from start?
11. Pointing: “Her only brother is my father.” How is speaker related to her? (male speaker)
12. Yesterday Monday. Tomorrow?

---

## ✅ Answers
1. **Follows**  
2. **Does not follow**  
3. **6 km North**  
4. **Maternal uncle**  
5. **Wife**  
6. Today=Sat → day after tomorrow=**Monday**  
7. Valid: 247, 248 → **2** (245 invalid)  
8. **25**  
9. **Does not follow**  
10. **13 km**  
11. Speaker is her **nephew** (or she is paternal aunt — male: she is aunt, he is nephew)  
12. Today=Tue? Yesterday Mon → today Tue; tomorrow Wed. Q incomplete — if “what is today?” → **Tuesday**
