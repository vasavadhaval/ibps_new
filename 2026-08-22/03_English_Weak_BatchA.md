# 📖 English Weak — Batch A | 22 Aug

> Collective nouns · Articles · Conjunctions  
> Target: **10+ / 12**

---

## Notes

### Collective nouns
- Team/jury **as one unit** → singular (*is*)  
- Members divided/disagree → plural often OK (*were divided*)

### Articles
- **a** university (/ju/ consonant sound)  
- **an** honest (/ɒ/ vowel sound)  
- **an** hour (silent h)

### Conjunctions
| Pair |
|---|
| Scarcely/Hardly … **when** |
| No sooner … **than** |
| Not only … **but also** |
| Either … **or** / Neither … **nor** |

---

## ✍️ Practice

1. The jury ___ divided in their opinion. (was/were) — members disagree → ?
2. The team ___ won the match. (has/have) — as unit → ?
3. He is ___ university student. (a/an)
4. She is ___ honest officer. (a/an)
5. Scarcely had he left ___ the phone rang. (when/then)
6. No sooner had the bell rung ___ students rushed out. (than/when)
7. Neither of the answers ___ correct. (is/are)
8. Find error: He is married with a doctor.
9. Find error: Scarcely had he entered then it rained.
10. Fill: I will call you ___ I reach home. (when/than)
11. ___ European country. (a/an)
12. The news ___ shocking. (is/are)

---

## ✅ Answers
1. **were** (divided opinions)  
2. **has**  
3. **a**  
4. **an**  
5. **when**  
6. **than**  
7. **is**  
8. with→**to**  
9. then→**when**  
10. **when**  
11. **a**  
12. **is**

---

> **Takeaway:** scarcely→when | no sooner→than | a university | an honest | news is
