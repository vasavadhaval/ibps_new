> Target: **12+ / 14**

---

## Quick rules
- **Stative:** know, believe, like → usually **no continuous** (*She knows*, not *is knowing*)
- **SVA:** One of / Each / Neither → **singular**
- **Phrase:** discuss (no about), despite (no of), tall enough
- **Idiom:** cut out **for** = suited for

---

1. Improve: She is knowing the answer.
