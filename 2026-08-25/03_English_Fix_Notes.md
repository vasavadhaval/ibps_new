## Rules (exam me seedha use)

### 1. Neither A nor B → verb nearer subject
- Neither Ramesh nor his **friends** ___ → **are**  
- Neither the boys nor the **girl** ___ → **is**

### 2. for vs since
- **for** + duration → for five years / for 2 hours  
- **since** + starting point → since 2020 / since Monday  

### 3. Hardly / Scarcely / No sooner
- Hardly had he spoken **when** … (not *then*)  
- No sooner … **than** …

### 4. Uncountables (singular feel)
- scenery, furniture, information, advice, luggage → **no “s”** usually  
- The **scenery** of Himachal **is** beautiful  

### 5. Verb + gerund (-ing)
- suggest / enjoy / avoid / mind / finish + **-ing**  
- She suggested **leaving** early (not *to leaving*)

### 6. Fixed prepositions
- capable **of** + -ing  
- good **at**  
- prevent + object + **from** + -ing  
- junior / senior / prefer **to** (not *than*)  
- order a coffee (not *ordered for*)  
- Return the documents (not *return back*)

### 7. Unless
- Unless = if not → **Unless you work hard** (not *Unless you do not*)

### 8. Idioms
| Idiom | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Once in a blue moon** | very rarely |
| At the drop of a hat | immediately |
| Blessing in disguise | good hidden in bad |

## ✍️ Drill (12) — choose error / fill

1. She has lived in Surat ___ five years. → **for**  
2. Hardly had he spoken ___ everyone clapped. → **when**  
3. Neither Ramesh nor his friends ___ present. → **are**  
4. She suggested ___ early. → **leaving**  
5. Error: The sceneries of Himachal are beautiful. → **The sceneries**  
6. Idiom for “very rarely”? → **Once in a blue moon**  
7. He is capable ___ handling pressure. → **of**  
8. He is junior ___ her manager. → **to**  
9. Unless you ___ work hard, you will fail. → **(just) work** / remove “do not”  
10. He prevented me ___ going there. → **from**  
11. Return ___ the documents. → (drop *back*)  
12. She ___ understand. → **doesn't** (not *don't*)
